Get the tracklist from an old 8tracks playlist

8tracks is gone, but the Wayback Machine kept copies of many of its playlists. Paste a playlist link and this page finds the saved tracklist.

Wayback Machine links and old share links like 8tracks.com/mixes/12345 work too.
